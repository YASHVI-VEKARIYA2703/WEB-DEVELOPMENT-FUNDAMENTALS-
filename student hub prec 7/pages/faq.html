Student Help & FAQs

Click on any question below to view the answer.

Q1. How do I download my exam hall ticket? +

You can download your exam hall ticket by logging into Student Hub and visiting the Examination / Timetable section 7 days prior to examinations.

Q2. What is the minimum attendance required to appear in exams? +

As per CHARUSAT University guidelines, a minimum of 80% aggregate attendance is mandatory across all enrolled courses to be eligible for end-semester examinations.

Q3. Where can I pay my semester fees online? +

Semester fees can be paid via net banking, UPI, or credit/debit card through the Fees tab on this portal, or in person at the University Accounts department.

Q4. How do I register for campus events and hackathons? +

Go to the Events page from the top navigation bar, browse the list of upcoming technical, cultural, or sports events, and click the 'Register for Event' button.

Q5. Whom should I contact for student portal login issues? +

If you forgot your password or cannot access your portal account, contact the IT Helpdesk at studenthub@gmail.com with your enrollment number.

Q6. How can I apply for a scholarship? +

Eligible students can submit government or university scholarship forms by visiting the Student Welfare Cell (Admin Block, Room 104) before the announced deadline.

Q7. Where can I view my semester results and grades? +

Navigate to the Result page from your Dashboard after logging in. You will be able to see your SGPA, CGPA, and subject-wise grade reports.
